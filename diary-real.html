This food diary helps me understand your eating patterns, energy, and how food makes you feel — over three days of normal life.

This is not about counting calories. It's about gentle observation: what you ate, when, and how you felt.

  • Log breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks for 3 days
  • Note mood and energy after meals
  • Daily water, sleep, movement
  • Bowel movements (optional but useful)
  • Any symptoms you notice

Your answers save automatically — you can fill this in across the 3 days as you go.

About 3-5 minutes per day.
